Frequently Asked Questions

How do employers calculate PTO accrual rates per paycheck?

Employers divide your total annual PTO allotment by your annual pay periods (e.g. 120 annual hours divided by 26 bi-weekly pay periods equals 4.62 hours of PTO accrued each paycheck).

What happens if I reach my company PTO accrual cap?

Once your balance hits the maximum accrual cap, additional hours stop accruing until you use banked PTO time, effectively forfeiting new vacation earnings.

Are unused PTO hours paid out when leaving a job?

In states like California, Illinois, Montana, and Nebraska, state law requires earned PTO to be paid out upon termination as earned wages. In other states, company policy dictates whether payout occurs.
